**Why it works (psychology used):**
- Name + program: personalization
- Real batch/seat count: scarcity
- Deadline on the offer: urgency + loss aversion
- "{X} learners started": social proof
- One-word reply "YES": lowest-friction CTA
- "Suits you": gives them control, no pressure
